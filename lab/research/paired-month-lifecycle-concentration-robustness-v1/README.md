# Paired-Month Lifecycle Concentration Robustness V1

This closed source-free Lab research family uses Lab-owned byte-equal copies of the fixed paired-month candidate's complete `1,428 / 1,428` selection lifecycle ledger and complete `54 / 54` June/July forward ledger. It resolves the naturally complete-data condition left open by invalid Unit 075 without reopening, executing or repairing that family.

The sole formal process ranks selection winners by doubled-cost lifecycle net within each of the five active components. It reports frozen top-5/top-10 gross-positive shares, removes exactly each component's top ten for a diagnostic residual, and compares all ten original-versus-residual component rank pairs. The forward is a descriptive global top-3/top-5 small-sample contrast only.

Selection is economically concentrated: four of five top-10 shares exceed `30%`, their median is `51.8291%`, four of five components become nonpositive after their own top ten are removed, and the union of 50 removed winners changes portfolio stressed net from `+$5,477.524` to `-$2,300.692`. However, all ten component rank pairs remain in the exact same order, so Kendall tau-a is `1.0`. Broad concentration passes, sign/rank fragility fails, and the strong robustness falsifier also fails. The frozen verdict is `AMBIGUOUS_SELECTION_COMPONENT_OUTCOME_CONCENTRATION`.

The forward warning is material but nonauthoritative for the primary verdict: the top three stressed winners are `29.5253%` of gross-positive forward value, and removing them changes full-forward stressed net from `+$30.626` to `-$8.878`. The top-five share is `42.9202%`, leaving `-$26.800`.

These are retrospective evidence-dependence diagnostics, not tradable winner-removal rules. The fixed development candidate, weights, risk, entries, exits and shared ladder remain unchanged. The result grants no component removal, reallocation, MT5, Lab engineering or Live authority.
