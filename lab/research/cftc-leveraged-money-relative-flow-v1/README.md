# CFTC Leveraged-Money Relative Flow V1

This active source-free Frontier family asks whether the latest causally
available CFTC Traders in Financial Futures report shows a leveraged-money
positioning shift toward Nasdaq mini rather than DJIA x $5 that is followed by
materially better US100-book economics relative to the US30 book.

The treatment is the weekly change in the difference between Nasdaq and DJIA
leveraged-money net position shares. It uses the two individual futures market
codes `209742` and `124603`; consolidated markets, absolute positioning levels,
other trader categories and magnitude thresholds are excluded.

Historical report dates are not treated as publication dates. Ordinary reports
become usable only on a server date strictly later than report date plus seven
calendar days. The documented 2023 ION and 2025 appropriations interruptions
use the CFTC's exact replacement publication dates, again only from a strictly
later server date. This deliberately conservative boundary prevents future
information and same-day release ambiguity.

Only outcome-free source and density feasibility has been inspected. It found
242 paired reports, 118 Nasdaq-shift and 123 Dow-shift changes, and full mapping
of 1,051 portfolio days with both states represented in every period. No CP2
close value, stressed return, stop association or book-relative verdict has
been read.

Official acquisition must be committed and pushed before the sole fixed
lifecycle aggregation. A complete pass may retain only one later,
independently declared whole-portfolio risk/lot treatment question. This unit
changes no entry, order, exit, priority, allocation, lot, risk, slot, component,
EA or Live behavior.
