# RC16 Feature Supply Decay V1

This frozen source-free Frontier family tests whether the observed decline in
native 13:30 RC16 signal supply from 2025 to completed 2026 months persists after
conditioning on the pre-decision volatility level.

The exact Unit 063 feature is reconstructed at every available native evaluation.
Nineteen fixed calendar months supply signal rate and median pre-decision
ATR/open. Annual rate ratio and partial correlation of calendar time with monthly
signal rate after removing log-volatility distinguish calendar decay from a
stable, volatility-conditioned supply.

The evidence spans only January 2025 through July 2026. It cannot prove that the
whole portfolio's turnover target is unreachable, select a month or volatility
regime, change RC16, or open an automatic new-signal candidate. No MQL, Tester,
broker, account, runtime, or Live surface is touched.
