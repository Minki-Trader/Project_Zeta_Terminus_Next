# Portfolio Native-Signal Supply Frontier V1

This source-free Frontier unit asks whether the portfolio's daily turnover
shortfall is an upstream native-signal supply problem whose low-supply days are
still economically suitable for future independent entry discovery.

The immutable four-period CP2 event paths provide the complete `SIZE_DAY`,
`SIGNAL_DECIDED`, admitted birth and final lifecycle roles. Structural
feasibility is frozen before close outcomes: 1,051 normal days produced 2,429
native passed signals and 2,233 durable births. Of the 994 lifecycle starts
missing from the three-per-day obligation, 918 remain missing even under
perfect same-day conversion of every observed native signal.

One fixed aggregation compares existing admitted lifecycles born on days with
fewer than three native signals against those born on days with at least three,
after component-period centering. This can decide only whether a later,
independently declared signal-discovery question is worth retaining. It cannot
impute an unobserved trade, repair Unit 061, change admission, risk, lot, slot,
clock or Live behavior, or claim that a new signal would inherit the value of
the observed fills.
