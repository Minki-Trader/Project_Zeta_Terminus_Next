# Pre-Decision Market Participation State V1

This frozen Frontier family tests whether broad broker H1 tick participation
immediately before the first native decision changes the existing portfolio's
lifecycle value and stop burden.

The treatment uses only the completed 11:00 server-time H1 bar for US30, US100
and US500, normalized by each symbol's preceding twenty same-clock bars. It is
known at 12:00, before the earliest 12:15 decision. The measure is broker quote
activity, not exchange volume or a direct liquidity claim.

One isolated Portable may acquire the three H1 volume paths. After that evidence
is frozen and pushed, one fixed source-free aggregation may join it to the
complete fixed-0.01 CP2 lifecycle population. No entry, order, exit, allocation,
lot, risk, slot, EA or Live behavior changes in this family.

The family closes after that bounded aggregation. It does not automatically open
another clock, lookback, volume field, price/volume interaction, symbol subset,
threshold, component, period, allocation or risk successor.
