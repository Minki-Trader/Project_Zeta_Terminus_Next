# Realized Scheduled Macro Shock Alignment V1

Unit `087` tests one bounded Program 2 question. For official CPI and
Employment Situation releases, it freezes the realized US30/US100/US500
price shock over the first fifteen minutes, then compares current native
lifecycles born strictly after that shock is known and before release plus
120 minutes.

The treatment is the sign of the median three-index fifteen-minute log
return. A later birth is `ALIGNED` when its direction matches that sign and
`OPPOSED` otherwise. The five births exactly at release plus fifteen minutes
are excluded so every included state is already observable before the birth.

One isolated Lab Portable may acquire only the required M5 event-window bars.
After those bars and their receipt are frozen and pushed, one fixed
source-free aggregation may consume the already frozen exploratory lifecycle
economics. This family changes no signal, order, exit, admission, priority,
lot, slot, risk, EA or Live behavior.

The family closes after that aggregation. No alternate release class, shock
window, magnitude threshold, symbol subset, event subset, birth window,
component, period, surprise field or same-family treatment opens
automatically.
