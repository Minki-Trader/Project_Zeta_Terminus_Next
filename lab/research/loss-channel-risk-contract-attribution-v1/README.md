# Loss Channel Risk Contract Attribution V1

This source-free Lab family owns closed Unit 066. It asks whether the portfolio's stop-defined risk budget also describes the loss mass that actually consumes capital, or whether most realized loss is discharged through native non-stop exits with a materially different planned-risk utilization distribution.

The unit has one finite three-lens bundle: complete CP2 actual/stressed loss-mass attribution by exit channel, full binding-run actual-loss confirmation from the immutable Tester deal table, and concentration plus planned-risk-utilization falsification. Stop protection remains a tail bound throughout; this unit cannot infer that non-stop losses are physically unbounded or authorize an exit/risk change.

The authoritative pre-outcome contract is `evidence/LOSS_CHANNEL_RISK_CONTRACT_ATTRIBUTION_DECLARATION_V1.json`. All inputs are immutable, previously consumed exploratory evidence. The unit adds no MQL, runtime, acquisition, Tester path, reusable CLI, broker/account query or Live action.
