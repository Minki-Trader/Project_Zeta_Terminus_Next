This source-free Lab family owns Frontier Unit 100 under Program 7 `diagnostics_causal_meta` at meso-to-macro height. It asks one bounded question raised by the closed native Optimization result: does the exact fixed-`0.01` virtual label rank the paired-month anchor's causal shared-account contribution, and can the same frozen feature/model form become informative when its label is replaced by prior-quarter realized doubled-cost R?

The full map was compared before opening the unit. Program 1's exact router has already been consumed and cannot receive an adjacent model/threshold rescue inside Optimization; Programs 2, 3, 4 and 5 have no equally ready nonadjacent current evidence. Program 7 alone has a newly complete causal mismatch: the router reduced reported DD to `12.52%` but destroyed more than `84%` of anchor profit with zero engineering fault. This unit diagnoses that mismatch without creating a trading candidate automatically.

Two related variants are frozen together:

1. `ORIGINAL_VIRTUAL_NET_RANK` reads the already-generated causal router rank and measures its association with matched anchor lifecycle actual/stressed R.
2. `PRIOR_QUARTER_ACCOUNT_STRESSED_R_RIDGE` keeps the exact ten features, pooled Ridge alpha `10`, 2%/98% label clipping, minimum `80` total and `8` per component, expanding memory, calendar-quarter fits and component-relative rank. Its only research change is the training label: matched anchor doubled-cost net divided by entry planned risk, available only after that lifecycle closes. Each quarter uses only closes from earlier quarters.

The exact router decision ledger and qualified anchor lifecycle ledger are copied once into a Lab-owned ignored input root and never read again from Optimization during formal analysis. Matching is fixed by component, entry minute, direction and feature tolerance. Warmup rows are disclosed but excluded from fitted-rank economic gates.

The account-R variant retains one later Optimization information seed only if at least `95%` of anchor closes match uniquely, at least `500` matched model-ready rows cover all five active components, pooled Spearman rank versus stressed R is at least `0.10`, at least four components have positive Spearman, the top-minus-bottom rank-quartile mean stressed-R gap is at least `0.10R`, and the bottom quartile has nonpositive stressed dollars over the full period and in at least three of four frozen epochs. Any failed gate closes both variants without another label, model, threshold, bin, component, epoch or feature rescue.

This family adds no MQL, SET, runtime, compile, Tester, test, validator or Live/Optimization mutation. Its output is diagnostic research information only and has no promotion authority.

## Result

The one formal process matched all `1,428 / 1,428` anchor closes uniquely to the `1,620` native router decisions. Input hashes and anchor actual/stressed totals matched, and duplicate keys, feature mismatches, unknown components, nonpositive planned risks and reconstructed-quarter mismatches were all zero. Fourteen of sixteen expanding quarter fits were ready, leaving `1,279` matched prospective rows across all five components.

The original virtual-net rank was already near zero against shared-account stressed R: pooled Spearman `0.01503277895231423`, bottom-quartile stressed `+$272.279`, and top-minus-bottom mean difference `0.012715988009001152R`. Replacing only the label with prior-quarter shared-account stressed R did not repair the form: pooled Spearman fell to `0.004564215461680821`, only three components were positive, the mean top-minus-bottom difference was `0.02540319499766008R`, and the bottom quartile remained profitable at `+$568.183` while nonpositive in only two of four epochs.

Coverage, sample count and component breadth passed. All five information/economic gates failed: pooled rank, positive-component breadth, quartile gap, full-period bottom-quartile loss and epoch persistence. No seed is retained. Both variants and every adjacent label, model, threshold, bin, feature, component, epoch or subgroup rescue are frozen, and the continuous Goal returns to a complete Optimization-map comparison.
