The campaign owns one-time byte-equal copies of the two paired-candidate lifecycle ledgers and both frozen source-result records. The sole formal process preserves every lifecycle and uses `actual - (multiplier - 1) × (actual - stressed_2x)`; it performs no parameter search, entry suppression, component retune, MQL change or MT5 run.

The `3x / 4x` books are closed-path arithmetic stresses. They do not claim linear broker costs, reproduce capital-ladder feedback or establish native open-equity drawdown. Exact already-completed native selection/forward drawdown remains disclosed separately.

This unit can strengthen or weaken development confidence in the already-fixed candidate, but it cannot select another candidate, mutate Lab or Live, or grant deployment authority.
